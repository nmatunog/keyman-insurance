3-Day Facilitated Program

3-DAY MENTOR CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

An immersive, hands-on workshop experience designed for agency leaders, unit managers, and sales coaches.

DAY 1 Experiential Learning

DISCOVER & VALIDATE

Experience the beginning of the journey.

  • SPIKE philosophy and complete journey overview
  • Mentor roles, mindset, and coaching fundamentals
  • Personal discovery and purpose alignment
  • Choose a target market & client personas
  • Prepare and conduct field validation interviews
  • Capture market insights and challenge assumptions
  • Real validation. Real experiential learning.
Mentor Takeaway: Step into the shoes of your participants so you can guide them with empathy and practical wisdom.
DAY 2 Canvas & Stage Gates

BUILD, TEST & PITCH

Turn evidence into a compelling business direction.

  • Process and analyze real market evidence
  • Build the Financial Entrepreneurship Canvas (FEC)
  • Connect all 10 components of the FEC model
  • Complete Version 1.0 business concept
  • Prepare a 5-minute sample business pitch
  • Stage-gate review, evaluation, and feedback
  • Learn to guide, not do the work for them
Mentor Takeaway: Build, refine, and pitch a real business concept using the exact discipline you'll teach others.
DAY 3 Execution & Scaling

DEVELOP, ACTIVATE & MULTIPLY

Equip them to practice, perform, and lead.

  • Support licensing and onboarding milestones
  • Mentor the client-advisory process
  • Assess through Advisor Revalida standards
  • 30-day activation planning and execution
  • Coach the Revenue Engine (10-5-3-1-3)
  • Introduce the Leadership Engine & multiplication
  • Handle difficult mentoring conversations
  • Build a practical mentor operating system
Mentor Takeaway: Learn how to develop capability, drive execution, and build future leaders who will multiply.

PROGRAM OUTCOME REFERENCE

The SPIKE Rookie Academy System (10-Week Participant Journey)

Workshop Context: Mentors who complete the 3-Day Workshop are fully prepared to guide their own rookies through this 10-week development curriculum.

Weeks 1–4 SEGMENT 1: DISCOVER AND BUILD

Focus: Purpose, Target Market & Business Blueprint
WEEK 1: Identity & Professional Discovery

Saturday: SPIKE philosophy, personal entry motivation, growth targets.

Weekday Assignment: Complete the Personal Vision, Income Aspiration, Professional Identity, and Initial Market Options worksheets. Conduct one mentor check-in.

WEEK 2: Target Market & Client Validation

Saturday: Target market personas & designing validation questions.

Weekday Assignment: Complete 5 real market-validation interviews in target segment.

WEEK 3: Value Proposition & Canvas Version 0.5

Saturday: Financial Value Proposition & core canvas structure.

Weekday Assignment: Develop FEC Version 0.5, focusing on target market, client problem, Financial Value Proposition, Client Experience, and Winning Strategy.

WEEK 4: Stage Gate - Business Blueprint Pitch

Saturday: 5-minute pitch presentation of completed FEC Version 1.0 before panel & mentor peers.

Weekday Assignment: Refine business model based on panel recommendations.

Weeks 5–7 SEGMENT 2: LICENSE, ONBOARD, AND ADVISE

Focus: Competence, Discovery & Advisory Process
WEEK 5: Licensing & Client Discovery

Saturday: Professional standards, ethics, mastery of discovery dialogs.

Weekday Assignment: Licensing exam completion & mock client discovery sessions.

WEEK 6: Financial Analysis & Solution Design

Saturday: Protection gap analysis, needs calculation & solution mapping.

Weekday Assignment: Practice 3 full financial needs analysis cases with squad peers.

WEEK 7: Stage Gate - Advisor Revalida

Saturday: Simulated advisory demonstration (Discovery → Presentation → Concern Handling → Closing).

Weekday Assignment: Complete any remaining licensing, onboarding, compliance, and practice-readiness requirements before client-facing activity.

Weeks 8–10 SEGMENT 3: EXECUTE, MULTIPLY, AND LAUNCH

Focus: Revenue Engine (10-5-3-1-3), Launch & Leadership
WEEK 8: The Revenue Engine (10-5-3-1-3)

Saturday: Operating rhythm formula, pipeline diagnosis & activity tracking.

Weekday Assignment: Build live prospect pipeline & conduct discovery meetings.

WEEK 9: Leadership Engine & Activation Plan

Saturday: Master → Multiply → Scale pathway, crafting 30-day activation plan.

Weekday Assignment: Finalize 30-day launch calendar with Unit Manager endorsement.

WEEK 10: Final Stage Gate - Graduation & Business Launch

Saturday: Active pipeline presentation & formal graduation.

Output Handover: Refined Financial Entrepreneurship Canvas and Business Launch Plan.
