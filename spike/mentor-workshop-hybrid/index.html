Hybrid Program Structure

HYBRID CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

Pre-work → Zoom → Field interviews → Live Day 1 → Live Day 2 (AM + PM Revalida).

PHASE 1 Pre-Work + Zoom (Remote)

DISCOVER, PREPARE & VALIDATE

Foundational learning and field preparation.

  • Complete pre-work modules: SPIKE philosophy, journey overview, mentor roles
  • Zoom session: Personal discovery, target market selection, interview prep
  • Coaching fundamentals and mentoring mindset
  • Assignment: Conduct real field validation interviews before Live Day 1
PHASE 2 Live Day 1 (In-Person)

BUILD, TEST & PITCH

Turn evidence into a compelling business direction.

  • Process and analyze real market evidence from field interviews
  • Build the Financial Entrepreneurship Canvas (FEC) — all 10 components
  • Complete Version 1.0 business concept
  • Prepare and deliver 5-minute sample business pitch
  • Stage-gate review, evaluation, and feedback from peers and facilitators
  • Learn to guide, not do the work for participants
PHASE 3 Live Day 2 (In-Person)

DEVELOP, ACTIVATE & FACILITATE

Morning: Execution & scaling. Afternoon: Full Facilitator Revalida.

Morning Session
  • Support licensing and onboarding milestones
  • Mentor the client-advisory process & Advisor Revalida standards
  • 30-day activation planning and execution coaching
  • Coach the Revenue Engine (10-5-3-1-3) and pipeline management
  • Introduce the Leadership Engine & multiplication pathway
Afternoon: Facilitator Revalida
  • Station 1 — Facilitate: Demonstrate facilitating a SPIKE session
  • Station 2 — Gate: Conduct a stage-gate review and provide feedback
  • Station 3 — Coach: One-on-one mentor coaching simulation
  • Station 4 — Ops: Demonstrate kit setup and operating system
  • Outcome: Pass = certified to facilitate 400–600 hr Rookie Academy with kit
Certification Outcome: Leaders who pass the afternoon Revalida leave certified as SPIKE Rookie Academy facilitators, equipped with workbook, rubrics, and full implementation kit.
